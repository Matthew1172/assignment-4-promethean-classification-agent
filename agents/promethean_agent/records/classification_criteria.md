# Significance criteria and counter-case

Course method: Mastering the Craft of Emerging Technology Adoption, Chapter 11, pp. 149–150, inspected locally on 2026-10-13. This is the author's working framework, not a claimed academic consensus.

First identify a usable production system. Then name the constraint and ask what disappears if the system is removed. A better process does not establish dissolution of a categorical human barrier.

| Criterion | Evidence needed in the cultivated-meat case |
|---|---|
| Order-of-magnitude improvement | Verified system-level comparison; no such improvement is established here. |
| Widespread impact | Durable effects beyond a niche food offering. |
| Paradigm shift | A demonstrated change in the relation between animal rearing and meat production. |
| Catalytic effect | Secondary innovations that depend on this production route. |
| Democratizing effect | Broad access rather than a premium-only ingredient. |
| Irreversibility | Durable dependence established over time; not observable from consultation records alone. |

The FDA records support a distinct production route. The reviewed evidence does not demonstrate food-scarcity dissolution. Strongest contrary case: the product remains a niche ingredient and removing it merely restores conventional sourcing. Do not force numerical scores from missing evidence. Prospective mode permits Potential Promethean at most, never Confirmed Promethean.
